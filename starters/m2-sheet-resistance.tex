\section{Derivation}
A doped line of length L, width W and thickness t, made from a layer of
uniform resistivity rho, has resistance

R = rho L / (W t)

The sheet resistance of the layer is Rs = rho / t, so

R = (rho / t) (L / W)

R = Rs L / W

\section{Example}
For Rs = 50 ohm per square, L = 200 um and W = 10 um, the line is
L/W = 20 squares long, so the equation above gives R = 1000 ohm.
